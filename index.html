Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need technical skills to work with you?

No. We explain everything in plain language and build with visual tools, so you can follow along and take over later.

Which businesses do you work with?

Founders, SMEs and B2B teams across Africa, in any sector. If you sell to businesses, or lose time to manual work, we can help.

Can you train my team instead of building for us?

Yes. The academy runs cohort classes, private workshops and 1:1 coaching in n8n, Make.com and Zapier, built around your own processes.

How does outbound lead generation work?

We define your ideal customer, build a prospect list with tools like Clay, Apollo.io and Snov.io, run personal outreach over email and LinkedIn, and pass the replies to your sales team.

How long does a project take?

Most automation projects run two to six weeks. We confirm the timeline and cost in the plan you receive before any build starts.
